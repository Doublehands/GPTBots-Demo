# Aurora Hotel Demo Design

## Goal

Update the travel-hotel solution page into a direct Aurora Hotel sales demo that shows how GPTBots, EngageLab, and Livedesk empower the full guest journey.

## Audience

The primary viewer is a Aurora Hotel business owner or operations lead. The page should feel like a practical solution walkthrough, not a generic hotel webpage.

## Page Logic

The hero positions the demo as a Aurora Hotel full-journey intelligent service and marketing solution. The copy explains the product split clearly:

- GPTBots handles AI Agent conversations, knowledge retrieval, personalized recommendations, workflow execution, and service intake.
- EngageLab handles APP Push, WebPush, SMS, WhatsApp, Email, and MA journey orchestration.
- Livedesk handles live agent handoff, service desk collaboration, ticket priority, and closure.

## Five Core Capabilities

The five-capability section becomes the main demonstration console:

- Direct booking conversion: GPTBots guides dates, rooms, policies, and booking next steps; EngageLab retouches abandoned or high-intent guests.
- Instant FAQ and facility support: GPTBots answers policies and facility questions from the knowledge base; Livedesk takes complex questions.
- Room and package recommendation: GPTBots compares room types and package options; EngageLab segments guests for follow-up.
- In-stay service and ticket closure: GPTBots collects service needs; Livedesk dispatches and tracks work; EngageLab writes behavior back into MA.
- Event, meeting, and member reactivation: GPTBots handles inquiry capture; EngageLab nurtures leads and repeat-stay campaigns.

## Visual Direction

Use a refined operational UI, closer to a sales console than a marketing hero. The capability area should include:

- A clearer section intro tied to Aurora Hotel.
- Capability tabs with product tags.
- A visual panel with the simulated guest conversation.
- A generated image replacing the current simulated conversation image slot.
- Product role cards for GPTBots, EngageLab, and Livedesk.

## Scope

Modify only the travel-hotel page template, page CSS, page JS, static generated asset, docs static export, and a lightweight static validation test.
